# Historical experiment bindings

These are unchanged metadata-only copies of the local consumed classifier and scope experiment markers. They record the original protocol and artifact/source hashes. No weights or raw examples are distributed here. They are provenance evidence, not runnable model artifacts; do not copy them into a new training run to claim it is the original experiment.

Original report files remain under `reports/`. Selected original development-run summaries, metrics, histories and figures are copied unchanged under `reports/experiments/`; their historical paths and status flags describe the stage when generated. Later Milestones 5–7 supersede earlier test-reservation statements. Preserve originals when starting new work.
